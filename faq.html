Clear aligners

Cost, timing and whether they suit you.

See what’s included on the aligners pricing section.

How long does aligner treatment take?

Aligner treatment usually takes about 10 to 14 months, depending on your case. You wear the trays around 22 hours a day, take them out to eat, and come in for regular reviews until you’re done.

Are clear aligners better than braces?

Aligners are nearly invisible, removable and easier to clean around, which many adults and students prefer. Braces can suit some bite problems better. Your doctor will tell you honestly which is right for you after an examination.

Compare them side by side on our aligners vs braces table.

How do I know if I’m a candidate for aligners?

Book a consultation. Your doctor will examine your teeth and gums and take a 3D scan, so no messy impressions. You’ll then know whether aligners suit you, what the plan looks like and what it costs.

Teeth cleaning

Scaling, polishing and how often to go.

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?

Most people benefit from a professional cleaning about every six months. Come sooner if your gums bleed, your breath is persistently bad, or you wear aligners or have implants.

What happens during a teeth cleaning?

Your doctor first checks your teeth and gums, then removes plaque and tartar with ultrasonic scaling, polishes away surface stains, and finishes with simple advice on brushing and flossing.

More detail is on our teeth cleaning page.

How much does teeth cleaning cost?

The cost depends on what your teeth and gums need, so we don’t quote a fixed price online. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll give you a written quote.

I have a missing tooth. Are dental implants right for me?

An implant replaces a missing tooth so it looks, feels and works like a natural one. Whether it suits you depends on your gums, bone and general health, which your doctor checks at a consultation. You then receive a written quote. Read more under dental implants.

Can a dentist help with snoring?
